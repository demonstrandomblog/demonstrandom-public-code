# Mineral-evolution data

The nine stage tuples and stage names in `persistence.py` are transcribed from
Table 1 of Robert M. Hazen and Michael L. Wong (2024), **Open-ended versus bounded
evolution: Mineral evolution as a case study**, PNAS Nexus 3(7), pgae248.
[Original article](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae248).

The source article is distributed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).
The original source's terms and attribution apply to its material; the repository's
original-code license does not replace them. Selected columns are reordered as
`(k_max, m, n, observed mineral count, observed information in bits)`, with shortened
stage labels. These are supplied source observations, not results generated by
this package. The code additionally uses the stated coefficient-permutation
correction factors for complexity 1 through 6.
